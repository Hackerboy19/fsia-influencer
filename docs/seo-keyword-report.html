FSIA Influencer Keyword Report

Checked 19 October 2026 against the live site, after the latest upload. On-page data comes from the live HTML and from rendering each page in a browser.

The technical foundation is now clean: all 338 automated checks pass on the live site. Keyword coverage is now the weak point. Pages are short, the home page shows almost no indexable text, and the phrases brands and creators actually search for (influencer marketing, Instagram, YouTube, city names, brand deals) barely appear.

What this report can and can't say. I have no Google Search Console, Keyword Planner or rank-tracker access, so there are no search volumes, rankings or click numbers here; none were estimated. Keyword priorities are based on search intent and on which competitors appear in web results today. The web search I used doesn't support site: queries, so whether Google has indexed the pages is not verified. Search Console is the only reliable source for that.

Action plan, in order

  1. Connect Google Search Console.

    Verify the domain, submit sitemap.xml, and wait for 2–4 weeks of query data. Real queries and impressions should replace the guesses in this report; everything below is easier to judge with them.

  2. Give the home page real text.

    Add 150–250 words below or beside the catwalk: who it is for (creators and brands in India), what happens after sign-up, the categories covered, and links to Join, Campaigns and Talent. Today Google sees 25 words.

  3. Rework titles and H1s as proposed above.

    This is the cheapest change with the most direct effect. It is a small edit in src/seo.ts and the page headings.

  4. Say the platforms and places out loud.

    Mention Instagram, YouTube and the cities where creators actually are (the roster lists Mumbai, New Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Kochi) in normal sentences on Join, Campaigns and Talent. Each currently appears 0–2 times.

  5. Expand the FAQ into full answers.

    Give GST, ASCI disclosure, micro-influencer eligibility and payment timing 80–150 words each, with facts the team can stand behind. These are real search topics with little commercial competition.

  6. Strengthen the brand entity.

    Add the official Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn profiles as sameAs in the Organization schema. Only real profile URLs; none were supplied, so none were added. Link the site from those profiles and from fsia.in.

  7. Later: individual creator pages.

    Profiles currently open as overlays with no URL of their own, so none can rank. A page per creator (/talent/name/) would target "[name] influencer" searches and would make ProfilePage markup eligible. Do this only once the roster has real, consenting creators.

  8. Hold off on category and city landing pages.

    With 6 creators, pages like "fashion influencers in Mumbai" would be thin or near-empty, and Google treats those as doorway pages. Revisit at roughly 10+ real creators per category or city.

Things to check before promoting the site

  • Roster accuracy. The listed creators use stock photos, and one is presented as "Winner, Miss India 2026". If these are sample profiles, label or replace them before targeting "hire influencers" or pageant searches. Presenting them as real could mislead brands and attract trust problems.
  • Claims in copy such as "100% secure escrow" and "payment within 24 hours" (listed in the audit report). Keyword pages built on claims the team can't back up tend to convert badly and invite complaints.
  • The old copy at fsia-influencer.vercel.app competes for the same brand searches and still shows the retired branding. Take it down or redirect it.

How this was measured

  • Titles, descriptions, headings and word counts: the live HTML of all 6 indexable pages, plus each page rendered in Chromium with its main text counted.
  • Term counts: whole-word matches in the rendered main content.
  • Roster: the live /api/creators response (3 sections, 6 creators).
  • Competitors: names that appeared in web search results for each cluster on 19 Oct 2026. This search is not Google India, so treat it as a sample of who is active, not as rankings.

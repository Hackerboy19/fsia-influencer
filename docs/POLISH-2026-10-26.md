## Problems found

1. **Logo mark was an "F"** (left over from the earlier name). It does not fit "Influencer Awards".
2. **Form borders too faint**: inputs and secondary buttons used `#CBC4B6` on white (about 1.7:1). Controls need 3:1 to be seen.
3. **Small and grey text**: secondary text `#625E56`, 12px uppercase labels, 13px helper text in forms and drawers.
4. **Home page did not say who it is for**: creators and brands had no clear path near the top. The brand path was at the very bottom, and the closing section repeated links that were already in the menu.
5. **Hero copy** ended with "The Influencer Award is separate.", which reads like a disclaimer. The mobile search placeholder was cut off.
6. **"How a profile gets here"** was a boxed four-card grid, the one card-style block left on the page.
7. **Form errors** appeared in one box at the bottom of the form, far from the field. On the long brand-enquiry drawer the message was off screen.
8. **Contact and booking forms** had no required markers, unlike the other forms.
9. **Drawers** (brand enquiry, campaign application, membership request) and the registration buttons still used the old style: pill close buttons, uppercase tracked buttons, large rounded cards and shadows.
10. **Claims the workflow does not support**: "Connect securely on WhatsApp for instant confirmation" (nothing confirms instantly); a guide excerpt said creators are "reviewed and verified" (there is no verification step); enquiry scope options said "Bollywood Gala".
11. **Join page** did not say what each piece of information is used for, or what happens after submitting. Half the desktop width was empty.
12. **Footer** had brand links mixed into "Help", and its text was 12–13px.
